# Privacy Policy

MC DLSS Prototype does not collect telemetry, analytics, account information, world data, screenshots, or gameplay data. It does not operate an update service and does not transfer information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.

The mod writes local configuration and diagnostic information inside the selected Minecraft instance. Minecraft, launchers, mod loaders, shader loaders, GPU drivers, GitHub, and separately obtained vendor SDKs or runtimes are independent third-party software governed by their own privacy policies.

Diagnostic logs may contain usernames, absolute file paths, hardware details, installed mod names, server addresses, or launcher information. Users must review and redact logs before posting them publicly. Complete launcher command lines must never be shared because they may contain account access tokens.

Questions about this policy can be raised through the repository's issue tracker. Security-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
